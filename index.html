How an order works

The whole process runs on written confirmations and bank records, so a buyer in Coimbatore or Hyderabad can order from Kerala without a visit.

Enquiry

Send grade, thickness, quantity and delivery town by WhatsApp, email or the form below.

Quote

Per sq ft ex-works, with sheets per truck and estimated freight. Valid 3 days.

Payment

Pay the proforma invoice by bank transfer (NEFT or RTGS) to the firm's current account.

Inspection and loading

Thickness, face and moisture checked and photographed at the mill. You or your agent can attend.

E-way bill and dispatch

Tax invoice with the mill's BIS licence number, e-way bill and LR sent to you before the truck leaves.

Mill prices, with the checks a mill will not do

We keep our margin low and fixed, so your landed cost stays close to the mill's own rate. In return, every load is chosen from the right mill, inspected before dispatch and backed after it leaves. A local dealer adds 15–25% for the same sheet.

Mill selection

Our sourcing is led by a plywood professional with thirty years in senior management positions at plywood factories across India. He knows each mill's glue line, core quality and real thickness, and matches your order to the right one.

Inspection at loading

Random sheets are measured with a digital gauge, faces checked for overlap and patches, moisture read on a pin meter. Photographs and readings reach you on WhatsApp before dispatch.

Mixed loads

One truck can carry 18 mm MR from one mill, 12 mm BWR from another and a bundle of flush doors from a third. You get one invoice and one e-way bill.

BIS on every invoice

Under the Quality Control Order only ISI-marked plywood may be sold in India. Our invoice carries the mill's CM/L licence number for every line, so your stock passes any inspection.

Replacement policy

Sheets found defective on arrival and reported with photographs within 48 hours are replaced in your next load, or credited. The mill's warranty is passed through to you in writing.

Papers that travel

Tax invoice, e-way bill, lorry receipt, mill test certificate and, for rubberwood timber, the forest transit pass, all sent as PDFs before the truck crosses the state border.

Questions buyers ask

Can I buy a part load or a few hundred sheets?

Our minimum is a full 22 ft truck, which can be a mix of grades and thicknesses. Smaller lots are possible when we are consolidating a truck to your city; ask and we will tell you the next date.

How can your prices be lower than a local dealer's?

We buy by the truckload direct from the mills, carry no godown stock, extend no credit and run a small team. That keeps our margin to a thin fixed amount per sheet, which is passed on as a lower price. A city dealer carries stock, credit and showroom costs and adds 15–25% for the same sheet.

Why no credit?

Our price is low because we carry no debtors. Dealers who want credit can usually arrange it with their own bank against our proforma invoice at a lower cost than any trader could build into the price.

How do I know the plywood is the grade I paid for?

Each line on the invoice carries the mill's BIS licence number and the IS standard. We send thickness readings and photographs before dispatch, and you or your agent are welcome at loading. Defects reported with photographs within 48 hours are replaced or credited.

Can you print my brand on the sheets?

Yes, for full truckloads of a single grade, through a mill that adds your brand to its BIS licence. Allow four to six weeks for the first branded load.

What documents travel with the truck?

Tax invoice with GSTIN and HSN 4412, e-way bill, lorry receipt, the mill's test certificate, and for rubberwood timber the Kerala Forest Department transit pass. All are sent to you as PDFs before dispatch.

Do you deliver outside South India, or export?

Yes. We deliver by road to any state in India; transit is 2–6 days for the north and east. Container exports of finished plywood, block board and doors are starting soon; write to sales@nexaply.in for export enquiries.
