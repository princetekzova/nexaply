BWR grade plywood

Boiling water resistant (BWR) plywood is bonded with a phenol-formaldehyde or phenol-modified resin, so the glue line survives repeated wetting. It is the grade most modular kitchen and bathroom vanity makers specify, and costs well below marine plywood.

Where it is used

  • Modular kitchens, including sink units
  • Bathroom vanities and utility areas
  • Semi-exposed furniture in coastal and humid regions
  • Shop fittings and display units

For permanent exterior exposure, boat building or bus and truck bodies, use BWP or IS 710 marine plywood.

What we check before loading

  • The dark phenolic glue line on the edge is the quickest visual check against MR stock sold as BWR.
  • Ask for the mill test certificate; we send it with the invoice.
  • Check that the face carries "BWR" with the ISI mark and licence number.

Questions buyers ask

BWR or BWP for a kitchen?

BWR is enough for most kitchens. Choose BWP if the kitchen is in a very humid coastal area or the sink cabinet is likely to see standing water.

Can BWR be used outdoors?

Only under cover. Unprotected outdoor use needs BWP or marine plywood, with sealed edges.
