MR grade plywood

Moisture-resistant (MR) plywood is the everyday interior grade: wardrobes, beds, cabinets, partitions and office furniture. Kerala mills make it with a rubberwood or hardwood core bonded with urea-formaldehyde resin, which gives a flat, stable panel at the lowest cost per sheet of any ISI-marked grade.

Where it is used

  • Wardrobes, beds and kitchen carcasses away from water
  • Partitions, false ceilings and wall panelling
  • Office, school and hotel furniture
  • Base board for laminates and decorative veneers

Not for areas that stay wet, or for exterior use. For kitchens under the sink, bathrooms and coastal sites, choose BWR or BWP.

What we check before loading

  • Check the ISI mark and CM/L licence number stamped on the face and printed on our invoice.
  • Measure thickness at several points; good 18 mm reads close to 18 mm, not 16.5.
  • Look at the edge: core veneers should be tight, without gaps or overlaps.
  • Tap the face: a hollow sound means a core gap or poor bonding.

Questions buyers ask

Is MR plywood waterproof?

No. MR plywood resists humidity and occasional splashes, but not soaking. It is meant for dry interiors.

Which thickness for wardrobes?

Usually 18 mm for carcass and shutters, 12 mm for internal shelves on short spans, and 6 mm for backs.
