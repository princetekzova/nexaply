Commercial and packing plywood

Packing plywood is a low-cost plywood used where appearance does not matter: export crates, machinery cases, pallet decks and boxes. Kerala mills supply it in thin thicknesses at the lowest price per square foot, mostly to exporters and factories in Tamil Nadu and Karnataka.

Specifications

BondingUF resin (MR) as standard
CoreRubberwood or mixed hardwood
FacesEconomy face; minor patches and colour variation allowed
Thickness4, 6, 9 and 12 mm (15, 18 mm on order)
Sizes8 × 4 and 6 × 4 ft; cut sizes for repeat orders
Export usePlywood is processed wood and exempt from ISPM-15; pallets with solid wood bearers are heat-treated

Where it is used

  • Export crates and machinery cases
  • Pallet decks and slip sheets
  • Fruit, fish and tea chests
  • Factory dunnage and site use

Packing plywood is not for furniture or construction. Plywood sold for those uses must be ISI-marked to the relevant standard.

What we check before loading

  • Tell us the end use, so we quote the right grade.
  • For repeat cut sizes, a mill can cut at the factory and save your workshop time.

Questions buyers ask

Is packing plywood ISI-marked?

Plywood for general use must carry the ISI mark under the Quality Control Order. We sell packing grade only for packing use and invoice it as such.

Minimum order?

One bundle can go in a mixed load; a full truck gets the best rate.

Need a full or mixed truckload?

Send grade, thickness, quantity and delivery town. We reply with an ex-works quote, valid for 3 days.
