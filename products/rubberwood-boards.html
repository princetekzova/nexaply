RUBBERWOOD

Rubberwood boards and sawn sizes

Rubberwood comes from rubber plantations in Kerala once the trees stop yielding latex. After chemical treatment and kiln-drying it is a pale, even-grained hardwood that machines and finishes well. We supply finger-joint and edge-glued boards and sawn sizes to furniture, kitchen and packing-case makers.

Where it is used

  • Furniture, beds and chairs
  • Kitchen shutters and countertops
  • Stair treads and handrails
  • Packing cases and pallets

Untreated rubberwood is attacked by insects; buy only treated and kiln-dried stock.

What we check before loading

  • Ask for the treatment certificate and check moisture with a pin meter.
  • Finger joints should be tight and staggered across the board.
  • Store under cover, flat and stacked with spacers.

Questions buyers ask

Is rubberwood strong enough for furniture?

Yes. Treated and kiln-dried rubberwood is a medium-density hardwood widely used for furniture in India and abroad.

Do you need a permit to move it?

Rubberwood from plantations has relaxed rules in Kerala; we arrange any transit pass needed for inter-state movement.

Need a full or mixed truckload?

Send grade, thickness, quantity and delivery town. We reply with an ex-works quote, valid for 3 days.
