IS 2202

Flush doors

Solid core flush doors are made from a block board core with hardwood lipping on all four edges and veneer faces, pressed as one panel. They are flat, strong and ready to paint, polish or laminate, and are bought in bulk by builders and contractors for apartments, hotels and institutions.

Other sizes are made to order from a door schedule. Doors are packed in bundles and can share a truck with plywood.

Where it is used

  • Internal doors in apartments and villas
  • Hotel, hospital and school projects
  • Bathroom doors in BWP grade
  • Laminated doors for offices

Main entrance doors exposed to rain need BWP grade with a sealed finish, or a solid timber door.

What we check before loading

  • Give us your exact sizes; doors are made to order and loaded in bundles.
  • Check the lipping is solid hardwood, so hinges and locks hold.
  • Ask for BWP grade for bathroom doors.

Questions buyers ask

Can you supply mixed sizes in one order?

Yes. Send a schedule of sizes and quantities; we load them with plywood or block board in the same truck.

Do the doors come with frames?

Frames are not standard. We can quote rubberwood or hardwood frames on request.

Need a full or mixed truckload?

Send grade, thickness, quantity and delivery town. We reply with an ex-works quote, valid for 3 days.
