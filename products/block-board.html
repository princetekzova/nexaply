Block board

Block board has a core of solid wood strips (battens) glued edge to edge and faced with veneers on both sides. It is lighter than plywood of the same thickness, does not sag on long spans, and holds screws well, which makes it the usual choice for long shelves, tabletops and doors.

Where it is used

  • Long shelves, bookcases and bed panels
  • Tabletops and counters
  • Doors and door frames
  • Wardrobe shutters over 7 ft

Block board is not suited to fine curved cutting or to wet areas in MR grade. Use BWP block board for kitchens.

What we check before loading

  • Look at the edge: battens should be close-fitting, about 25 mm wide, without large gaps.
  • Check the board for flatness on a level surface.
  • Choose rubberwood core for screw-holding and pine core for lighter weight.

Questions buyers ask

Block board or plywood for a 6 ft shelf?

Block board. Its solid battens resist sagging over long spans better than plywood of the same thickness.

Can block board take laminate?

Yes, both faces are veneered and sanded for laminate or veneer.
